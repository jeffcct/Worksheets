\noindent These questions are extra practice in the same style as Questions 1 and 2 of the
\textit{Linear number patterns} worksheet: a picture pattern grows by the same amount each
step, so the number of items follows a linear rule. For each question:
(a) draw the next pattern, (b) complete the table, (c) find a formula, then (d)--(e) use your
formula to answer questions about much larger patterns.

\section*{Question 1 -- Matchstick Squares}

This pattern shows the number of matchsticks needed to make a row of joined squares.

\noindent\textbf{1:}\;\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.78, line width=0.9pt, baseline=-0.5ex]
  \draw (0,0) -- (1,0) -- (1,1) -- (0,1) -- cycle;
  \foreach \p in {(0,0),(1,0),(1,1),(0,1)} \fill \p circle (2.2pt);
\end{tikzpicture}\\[3pt]
\textbf{2:}\;\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.78, line width=0.9pt, baseline=-0.5ex]
  \draw (0,0) -- (1,0) -- (1,1) -- (0,1) -- cycle;
  \draw (1,0) -- (2,0) -- (2,1) -- (1,1);
  \foreach \p in {(0,0),(1,0),(2,0),(0,1),(1,1),(2,1)} \fill \p circle (2.2pt);
\end{tikzpicture}\\[3pt]
\textbf{3:}\;\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.78, line width=0.9pt, baseline=-0.5ex]
  \draw (0,0) -- (1,0) -- (1,1) -- (0,1) -- cycle;
  \draw (1,0) -- (2,0) -- (2,1) -- (1,1);
  \draw (2,0) -- (3,0) -- (3,1) -- (2,1);
  \foreach \p in {(0,0),(1,0),(2,0),(3,0),(0,1),(1,1),(2,1),(3,1)} \fill \p circle (2.2pt);
\end{tikzpicture}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
  \item Draw the next pattern (4 squares in a row).
  \drawbox{2.8cm}
  \item Copy and complete the table.

  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
    \hline
    $S$ & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 12 \\
    \hline
    $M$ & & & & & & \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}

  \item Construct a formula for the number of matches ($M$) needed for any number of squares ($S$).
  \anshere
  \item If a row had 40 squares, how many matches would be needed?
  \anshere
  \item If a row had 75 squares, how many matches would be needed?
  \anshere
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Question 2 -- Hexagonal Picnic Tables}

A campsite joins hexagonal picnic tables end to end in a row, as shown below (each dot is a
seat). When two tables are pushed together, the shared side can no longer seat anyone.

\noindent\textbf{1:}\;\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.60, line width=0.9pt, baseline=-0.5ex]
  \draw (0.87,0.5) -- (0,1) -- (-0.87,0.5) -- (-0.87,-0.5) -- (0,-1) -- (0.87,-0.5) -- cycle;
  \foreach \p in {(0.43,0.75),(-0.43,0.75),(-0.87,0),(-0.43,-0.75),(0.43,-0.75),(0.87,0)}
    \fill \p circle (2pt);
\end{tikzpicture}\\[3pt]
\textbf{2:}\;\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.60, line width=0.9pt, baseline=-0.5ex]
  \draw (0.87,0.5) -- (0,1) -- (-0.87,0.5) -- (-0.87,-0.5) -- (0,-1) -- (0.87,-0.5) -- cycle;
  \draw (2.6,0.5) -- (1.73,1) -- (0.87,0.5) -- (0.87,-0.5) -- (1.73,-1) -- (2.6,-0.5) -- cycle;
  \foreach \p in {(0.43,0.75),(-0.43,0.75),(-0.87,0),(-0.43,-0.75),(0.43,-0.75),
                  (2.16,0.75),(1.30,0.75),(1.30,-0.75),(2.16,-0.75),(2.6,0)}
    \fill \p circle (2pt);
\end{tikzpicture}\\[3pt]
\textbf{3:}\;\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.60, line width=0.9pt, baseline=-0.5ex]
  \draw (0.87,0.5) -- (0,1) -- (-0.87,0.5) -- (-0.87,-0.5) -- (0,-1) -- (0.87,-0.5) -- cycle;
  \draw (2.6,0.5) -- (1.73,1) -- (0.87,0.5) -- (0.87,-0.5) -- (1.73,-1) -- (2.6,-0.5) -- cycle;
  \draw (4.33,0.5) -- (3.46,1) -- (2.6,0.5) -- (2.6,-0.5) -- (3.46,-1) -- (4.33,-0.5) -- cycle;
  \foreach \p in {(0.43,0.75),(-0.43,0.75),(-0.87,0),(-0.43,-0.75),(0.43,-0.75),
                  (2.16,0.75),(1.30,0.75),(1.30,-0.75),(2.16,-0.75),
                  (3.89,0.75),(3.03,0.75),(3.03,-0.75),(3.89,-0.75),(4.33,0)}
    \fill \p circle (2pt);
\end{tikzpicture}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
  \item Draw the next pattern (4 hexagonal tables joined in a row).
  \drawbox{3.0cm}
  \item Copy and complete the table below.

  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
    \hline
    $T$ & 1 & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 25 \\
    \hline
    $C$ & & & & & & \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}

  \item Construct a formula for the number of seats ($C$) needed for any number of tables ($T$).
  \anshere
  \item If the campsite joined 40 tables in a row, how many seats would there be?
  \anshere
  \item If the campsite needed to seat 70 people in a single row of joined tables, how many
    tables would be needed?
  \anshere
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Question 4 -- Garden Fencing}

A fence is built from posts with two horizontal rails running between each pair of neighbouring
posts, as shown below.

\noindent\textbf{1:}\;\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.72, line width=0.9pt, baseline=-0.5ex]
  \foreach \x in {0,1} {
    \draw (\x,0) -- (\x,1.4);
    \fill (\x,1.4) circle (2.2pt);
  }
  \draw (0,0.4) -- (1,0.4);
  \draw (0,1.0) -- (1,1.0);
\end{tikzpicture}\\[3pt]
\textbf{2:}\;\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.72, line width=0.9pt, baseline=-0.5ex]
  \foreach \x in {0,1,2} {
    \draw (\x,0) -- (\x,1.4);
    \fill (\x,1.4) circle (2.2pt);
  }
  \draw (0,0.4) -- (2,0.4);
  \draw (0,1.0) -- (2,1.0);
\end{tikzpicture}\\[3pt]
\textbf{3:}\;\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.72, line width=0.9pt, baseline=-0.5ex]
  \foreach \x in {0,1,2,3} {
    \draw (\x,0) -- (\x,1.4);
    \fill (\x,1.4) circle (2.2pt);
  }
  \draw (0,0.4) -- (3,0.4);
  \draw (0,1.0) -- (3,1.0);
\end{tikzpicture}

\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*)]
  \item Draw the next pattern (5 posts).
  \drawbox{2.8cm}
  \item Copy and complete the table below.

  \begin{center}
  \begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|c|c|c|}
    \hline
    $P$ & 2 & 3 & 4 & 5 & 6 & 7 \\
    \hline
    $R$ & & & & & & \\
    \hline
  \end{tabular}
  \end{center}

  \item Construct a formula for the number of rails ($R$) needed for any number of posts ($P$).
  \anshere
  \item If a fence had 50 posts, how many rails would be needed?
  \anshere
  \item If a fence used 200 rails, how many posts would it have?
  \anshere
\end{enumerate}
